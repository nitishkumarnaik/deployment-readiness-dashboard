## Summary
Kept stores everything you enter **only on your device**. We do not run servers for Kept, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal or health data.

## What the app stores on your device
- Profile: first name (optional), medication type and schedule, current dose (as you typed it), units, weights, protein goal, workout preferences.
- Logs: doses, protein entries, daily check-ins (nausea, fatigue, appetite), weights, workouts.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

## What we collect
Nothing. Kept contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Sharing
Only you can share your data, for example by using "Share summary" to send a clinician summary through your phone's share sheet. What happens after that is governed by the app you share it with.

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, Kept schedules **local** notifications on your device. No push service or server is involved.

## Backups
Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include app data, under your device settings and Apple's or Google's policies.

## Deleting your data
Settings → "Delete all data" removes everything immediately. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## Children
Kept is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Medical disclaimer
Kept is a general-wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, or dosing recommendations.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
