# Store listing copy (draft)

Avoid medication brand names (Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, Zepbound®…) in the title, subtitle, keywords and screenshots: they are trademarks, and both stores reject listings that misuse them. Generic names are fine.

## App Store (iOS)

**Name (30):** Kept: GLP-1 Protein & Strength
**Subtitle (30):** Lose the weight, keep muscle
**Promotional text (170):** New: dose-cycle intelligence. Kept learns which days after your dose you feel best and plans your strength sessions around them.
**Keywords (100):** glp1,semaglutide,tirzepatide,protein tracker,muscle,weight loss,workout,injection,shot log,dose

**Description:**

Losing weight on GLP-1 medication? Make sure it's fat, not muscle.

Kept is a simple companion for people taking semaglutide, tirzepatide and other GLP-1 medications. It helps you do the two things clinicians most often recommend to protect your muscle while the scale goes down: eat enough protein and do some strength training. It fits around how you actually feel.

PLANS THAT FOLLOW YOUR DOSE CYCLE
Side effects often follow a weekly pattern. Kept learns yours from a 10-second daily check-in and puts your strength sessions on your better days, with a rest day when side effects usually peak.

A PROTEIN FLOOR, NOT A CALORIE CEILING
No food database, no calorie counting. One number a day, logged in a tap: +25 g shake, +30 g chicken. On low-appetite days you get easy, protein-dense ideas.

20-MINUTE HOME STRENGTH WORKOUTS
Three short bodyweight or light-dumbbell workouts. Every move has a low-energy version for rough days, and it still counts.

YOUR MUSCLE SCORE
One weekly number you control, unlike the scale: protein days, strength sessions and check-ins.

CLINICIAN SUMMARY
A one-page summary of your doses, side effects, weight, protein and training to share at your next appointment.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your data stays on your phone.

Kept is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not give medical advice or recommend doses. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
